INSTRUCTIONS FOR INPUT_DATA SHEET:
================================

1. YELLOW CELLS: These are inputs that you should modify based on your scenario
2. WHITE CELLS: These are calculated or informational
3. Make sure all values are in consistent units
4. Technology efficiency should be 0 to 1 (or >1 for heat pumps)
5. Discount rate should be as decimal (0.05 = 5%)
6. Capacity factors should be 0 to 1
7. Emission factors should be in kg CO2 per unit of energy
